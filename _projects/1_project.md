## Overview

The **Analytics AI Copilot** is an agentic AI system designed to make data analysis more accessible through natural-language interaction.

The project formed part of my MSc research in Data Science and Analytics at Academic City University. It explores how AI agents can support analysts and decision-makers by interpreting user questions, interacting with data, performing analytical tasks, and presenting insights in an understandable form.

## Key Focus Areas

- Agentic AI
- Conversational analytics
- Natural language processing
- Automated data analysis
- Decision support
- Responsible AI

## Motivation

Traditional analytics workflows often require users to understand databases, analytical tools, or navigating through several dashboards. The goal of this project was to explore how an intelligent AI copilot could reduce this barrier and allow users to interact with data more naturally.

The work was presented at **AI for Tomorrow** and **IndabaX Ghana**.
