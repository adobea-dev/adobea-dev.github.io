Dealer AI Copilot

An agentic AI copilot designed to make business and dealership analytics more accessible through natural-language interaction and automated data analysis.

Overview

The Dealer AI Copilot was my final-year research project for my MSc in Data Science and Analytics at Academic City University, Ghana.

The research explored how agentic AI and large language models can be used to make analytics more accessible, conversational, and actionable for decision-makers.

The motivation behind the project was to reduce the technical barrier between business users and their data. Instead of relying entirely on analysts, dashboards, SQL queries, or complex analytical tools, the Dealer AI Copilot allows users to interact with data using natural language and receive useful analytical insights to support decision-making.

Research Focus

My research focused on the development of an AI-powered analytics copilot capable of interpreting user requests, reasoning about analytical tasks, interacting with data, and returning understandable insights.

The project explored how an AI agent could move beyond simple question answering and perform multi-step analytical workflows on behalf of a user.

Key research areas included:

  • Agentic AI
  • Large Language Models
  • Conversational analytics
  • Natural-language interaction with data
  • Automated data analysis
  • Statistical and predictive analytics
  • Business intelligence
  • Decision-support systems
  • Responsible AI

Dealer-Focused Analytics

The system was designed around a dealership use case, where users could interact with business and operational data conversationally.

Rather than manually navigating multiple dashboards or performing complex queries, a dealer could ask analytical questions in natural language and use the copilot to explore their data and support business decisions.

The research investigated how an intelligent copilot could serve as an interface between non-technical business users and complex analytical workflows.

Agentic AI Approach

A major component of the project was the use of agentic AI.

Instead of treating the large language model as a simple chatbot, the Dealer AI Copilot was designed around an agent-based workflow where the system could interpret a user’s objective, determine the analytical steps required, interact with relevant tools or data, and generate a response based on the results.

This approach enables the system to support more complex analytical requests than traditional conversational interfaces.

Research Motivation

Organizations generate large volumes of data, but accessing meaningful insights from that data often requires technical expertise.

Business users may depend on analysts to answer questions, build dashboards, generate reports, or perform ad-hoc analysis. This can create delays between asking a business question and receiving an actionable answer.

My research explored whether an agentic AI copilot could help close this gap by providing a more intuitive interface for analytics while still supporting responsible and data-driven decision-making.

Recognition

The research formed part of my MSc in Data Science and Analytics, where I graduated with First Class honours.

I received the Best Student in Computational Science and Informatics Award, with my research focused on the development of the Dealer AI Copilot.

The work has also been presented at technology and AI events, including AI for Tomorrow and IndabaX Ghana, where I discussed the application of agentic AI to analytics and decision support.

Impact

The Dealer AI Copilot reflects my broader research interest in developing AI systems that are not only technically capable but also practical, responsible, understandable, and useful to real users.

The project demonstrates how agentic AI can potentially transform the way organizations interact with data by making sophisticated analytics more accessible through natural-language interfaces.
