My work sits at the intersection of **data science, agentic AI, applied machine learning, responsible AI, and decision-support systems**. I am particularly interested in research that moves beyond prototypes and demonstrates how AI can be applied responsibly to real business and societal problems.

## MSc Research

### Dealer AI Copilot — Agentic AI for Conversational Analytics

My MSc research at **Academic City University** explored how agentic AI and large language models can make analytics more accessible to business users.

The **Dealer AI Copilot** enables users to interact with dealer performance, sales, leads, applications, and operational data through natural-language queries. The research investigates how AI agents can interpret analytical requests, interact with data and analytical tools, and return useful insights for decision-making.

**Research areas:** Agentic AI · Large Language Models · RAG · Conversational Analytics · Statistical Modelling · Decision Support

**Recognition:**

- Best Computational Science and Informatics Student Award

## Applied Research

### National Flood Command Center — CiviSenti

As part of my applied AI research work, I worked on **CiviSenti**, a community-driven flood-intelligence capability within the National Flood Command Center platform.

The system transforms community flood reports submitted through WhatsApp into structured information and combines those observations with rainfall and flood-risk data to support monitoring and validation.

My work included components for report processing, validation, workflow automation, testing, and integration with the broader flood-intelligence platform.

**Research areas:** Applied AI · Climate Resilience · Community Intelligence · Data Validation · Responsible AI
